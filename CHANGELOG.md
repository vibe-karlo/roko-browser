# Changelog

All notable changes to roko. The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/),
and versions follow [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/).

## [Unreleased]

### Added
- Split View: two tabs on screen at once, one above the other (after VerticalSplit and Amie). Start it from the page menu, by typing "split" in the bar, or from a tab's card in the switcher (touch and hold, Open in Split View). The two pages are rounded cards on a thin dark band; drag the band anywhere and it stays where you let go; drag it nearly shut and the other tab has the screen again. Starting Split View from the page menu or the bar splits the screen at once: your page on top, and below it a card, Open below, with a search field and your open tabs to tap. Typing "split" in full makes Split View the bar's first row. The bar works the half you touched last; the other is dimmed a little. The handle has Swap and Close. A tab chosen from elsewhere takes the place of the half in use.
- Touch and hold a link for its page live in a preview, as in Lens: the page loads in the preview, and tapping it opens the page as a tab, where it was. The menu under it has Open in New Tab, Open in Background (next to this tab, for later), Open in Private Tab, Add to Read Later, Copy Link and Share. Settings → Tabs → Peek at links turns it off.
- Forms stay safe. A form you've typed into (more than a few letters, or three fields) keeps its tab from being closed as unused or put to sleep, and if the page crashes or roko is closed, what you typed comes back into its fields when the page loads again, with "Brought back what you typed". Passwords, card numbers, one-time codes and search boxes are never kept; private tabs keep nothing on disk. It's forgotten once the form is sent or you go to another page, and after a week.
- Lock private tabs and history (Settings → Privacy): private tabs and your history show only after Face ID or the passcode, from each time roko opens. A locked private tab shows a lock over it, in the app switcher too; its card in the tab switcher has no picture or name; the bar hides your history and private tabs, with one row to unlock them. A private tab you open yourself while it's locked shows at once.
- A mini player for sound and video in a tab you've left, after Lens: the page lifts into a card with rounded corners and the player sits in a slim dark band under it, one row: play and pause, what's playing with its site and small level bars that move while it plays, 15 seconds back and on, Picture in Picture for video and AirPlay. Tap the title to go back to the tab; hold it for Full Screen or Stop. Pull it down and it shrinks to a thin strip that keeps playing; tap the strip or pull it up for the player again, pull the strip down to stop. Picture in Picture brings the video's tab up for a moment (iOS only starts it for a video on screen), and once the video has its own window you're back on the page you were reading; while it floats, the band steps aside.
- Downloads: files from pages go to roko's Downloads folder, also in the Files app under On My iPhone → roko, and are listed in the page menu (Downloads) or by typing "downloads". Each shows its progress, with pause and resume; a finished one is offered above the bar with Open (Quick Look, which can share or save it elsewhere). A file keeps downloading for as long as iOS lets roko run in the background, and one stopped there or by a dropped connection picks up where it stopped once roko is back. Two files of one name don't overwrite each other. A tab opened only for a file closes itself. Boarding passes and tickets still go straight to Wallet.
- Copy as Markdown, in the page menu or by typing "markdown": the article as clean Markdown (title, address, headings, paragraphs, lists, quotes, code and links), for notes or an AI chat.
- Swipe anywhere on a page, not only from its edge, to go back or forward, with an arrow that slides in and fills when letting go would go. A swipe that starts on something that moves sideways itself (a carousel, a map, a slider, a field) is left to the page. Settings → Tabs turns it off.
- Pinch a page in to see your tabs. Settings → Tabs turns it off.
- roko from outside it: New Tab, New Private Tab and Ask roko in Control Center, on the Lock Screen and on the Action button; the same, and Open in roko, in Shortcuts; and "New tab in roko", "Ask roko" to Siri.
- Reopen a closed tab. Throw a tab away in the switcher and its site shows above the bottom row with Reopen: one tap brings it back where it was, with its back and forward history. Typing "reopen" in the bar does the same for the last ten closed while roko is open. Private tabs aren't kept.
- Links come out clean. Copy Link and Share in the page menu leave out what only says where a link came from: utm_ tags, fbclid, gclid, Instagram's igsh, YouTube's and Spotify's si, X's s and t, Amazon's pd_rd_ and pf_rd_, and the like. What changes what opens (a video's time, a page number, a product's size) stays.
- Open in app: on a page whose app you have (YouTube, Instagram, Spotify, Reddit, X, Google Maps and about thirty more), roko offers to open it there, above the bar, once per app while roko is open. Settings → Tabs → Open links in apps chooses Offer, Always (a link you tap to such a page goes straight to the app, as in Safari; moving around inside one site stays in roko) or Off.
- Ask roko, first in the menu over text you select on a page: Browse for Me on those words.
- roko in other apps' share sheets: Open in roko, with roko's mark, in the list of actions (as Arc's Open in Arc Search), and roko among the apps at the top. Share a page from Safari, Mail or any app and it opens in roko; share words you selected (from Notes, say) and Browse for Me looks them up. A link pasted as text opens as a link.
- Sites that ask for a user name and password before they load (routers, printers, some work tools, staging sites) get a sign-in dialog, titled with the site's name and saying when the connection isn't private. A wrong password asks again, up to three times; the sign-in lasts while roko is open, and iOS offers to keep it in Passwords.
- Sun, a new app icon in Settings → App icon: a black sun with tapered rays and an open centre, on orange.
- YouTube plays on as sound when you leave roko or lock the iPhone, so a talk or a song keeps going in your pocket. It shows in the Dynamic Island and on the Lock Screen as Now Playing, with pause, play and the time left. Only what was playing as you left plays on: a paused video stays paused, and a pause from the Lock Screen holds. A page's sound now also plays with the Ring/Silent switch at silent, as in Safari.
- Hide Distracting Items, as in Safari: in the page menu, tap something on the page to mark it, tap it again and it fades away, and it stays hidden on that site from then on, from the first paint. The bar shows how many are hidden, with Undo for the last one and Done. A tap picks the smallest real block under your finger (a button, not the row it sits in), and an empty wrapper around it goes with it. The page menu shows how many are hidden on a site; Site Settings has Show Hidden Items, and Settings → Sites lists and resets them. It costs nothing on pages: what you hid travels with the hiding answer every page already gets, and the picker is put into a page only while you're using it. Names a site generates afresh (hashed classes) aren't relied on, so a hidden thing stays hidden after the site updates.
- Pages open the moment you press Go. While you type in the bar, the site its top row would open starts loading out of sight (as Safari's Preload Top Hit does), drawn in place behind everything, so Go shows a page already laid out instead of a black and then a white screen. Measured in the simulator: about 1.3 seconds from Go to the first picture before, under 0.1 seconds now. It waits for a quarter-second pause in typing, only for a site (never a search), only for a new tab, and the page is dropped if you close the bar or type something else. The spare web view new tabs start from is built again after each one is taken, not only at launch.

### Fixed
- A sign-in or other error a site answered with no page (an empty 401 or 404 of no type) came up as a file to save, named after the address. It shows as the site's page now.
- Saving an image from a page (long-press, Save to Photos, or Share and Save Image) closed roko: it never said why it adds to Photos, and iOS ends an app that asks without saying. It asks once now, and the image is saved. Pages that take a photo for an upload say why too.
- Cookie pop-ups whose only no costs money ("Reject and Pay", "Pay to Reject", "Reject and Purchase...", as on Reach's sites: the Mirror, Manchester Evening News, WalesOnline) are walls now: roko accepts them, as it does other sites that don't work without, instead of pressing reject and landing on the payment page, or leaving the pop-up up when its rule couldn't press anything.
- Read Later: save a page from its tools, or type "save for later" in the bar; "read later" opens the list. The page's text is kept as it was saved, so it can be summarized offline or after the page changes. Each page shows its picture, title, site and reading time; tap to read it (it counts as read), swipe to remove it or mark it read. Pages on one subject are gathered under its name ("Coffee and caffeine") with Summarize beside it, and Summarize all does every unread page: one summary by theme, each point marked with the page it comes from, which opens it. Apple Intelligence writes it, then Claude, then the on-device model, each within its reading limit; long pages are shortened, and with many pages each is read down to its main points first.
- Read Later syncs with the Mac: the Raycast extension has Read Later (open, mark read, remove, open in roko on the iPhone) and Save for Later (the tab in front, or a link). A page saved on the Mac is read on the iPhone when it arrives. The server needs `npm run deploy` for it; until then the iPhone keeps syncing everything else.
- The Eclipse, roko's circle mark, is the app icon now, in the app's own cream on near-black, with a dark version and a tinted one for Home Screen tinting. The grey star it replaces is in Settings → App icon. The Raycast extension has the same mark, on a rounded tile sized like a Mac app icon.
- Sync with your Mac, through roko's own server: to-dos and the three shopping lists the same on the iPhone and on the Mac with roko's Raycast extension (Raycast/), and links sent from the Mac ("Send to roko", the front tab in Safari, Chrome, Arc, Brave or Edge) opening in roko as tabs. Turn it on in Settings → Sync with your Mac and copy the key into Raycast. While roko is open a change on the Mac shows within about four seconds; closed, roko catches up when opened. Things added on the Mac are sorted into their aisle by roko. When both sides change one thing, the later change wins. roko's server now runs at roko-share.roko-browser.workers.dev, on Cloudflare's free plan, so Share in Browse for Me is on too. Push to wake roko comes with the paid Developer Program.
- The README shows the app: six screenshots (the bar, Browse for Me, a recipe summary, picks, videos and a clean page) and what roko does, in five short parts.
- A summary keeps the page as a miniature: once the summary arrives, the page shrinks whole into a small thumbnail beside its title and site, instead of a strip cropped to the site's header. Tap the miniature or the title to go back; the page grows out of it.
- Browse for Me looks on YouTube too. For anything more than a quick fact, the top videos on the question show under the answer (frame, length, title, channel and views), each opening on YouTube in a new tab. What the videos say about themselves (their descriptions, where recipes, steps and chapters usually are) is read alongside the web pages: Apple's model takes the two most informative as sources, and Claude gets the top three with the question and may point to one worth watching. It runs beside the web search, so answers aren't slower; Claude waits at most 1.5 seconds for it. No key or account: it's the request YouTube's own search box makes. Transcripts aren't read: YouTube now gives them only to its own player.
- Choose the languages pages are translated into (Settings → Translate; about thirty). The page menu offers the one used last; with more than one chosen, touch and hold it for the others, marked by a small chevron. Until you choose, it's English and the phone's own language.
- Shopping adds to the list showing: on Drogerija or Ljekarna, what you type or say goes there. On Trgovina, something usually bought elsewhere still goes on Trgovina, with a one-line offer above the bar ("Flasteri obično Ljekarna · Premjesti"); one tap moves it, and it goes there from then on. Added from the address bar, things still go to their usual shop.
- Swipe left or right on the shopping list for the next shop's list; it slides in from the side you swiped.
- Shopping has three lists, a tap apart at the top: Trgovina (groceries), Drogerija (dm, Bipa) and Ljekarna (pharmacy), each with how much is left on it. Things go to the right one by themselves: "šampon" and "pelene" to the drugstore, "Brufen", "sirup za kašalj", "vitamin C" and "flasteri" to the pharmacy (a new Medicines and health aisle), the rest to groceries. Long-press one for Move to Shop, and it goes there from then on. Added from another list, a toast says where it went ("Šampon → Drogerija"). Suggestions are for the list showing.
- Say a shopping list: the microphone in Shopping's bar listens, shows what it hears, and adds it all after a pause (or Gotovo). Spoken lists have no commas, so they're split on the things you buy and the everyday ones, the longest first: "mlijeko jaja kruh i banane" is four, "pileća prsa crveni luk i češnjak" three, "dvije banane i kilo jabuka" Banane and Jabuke. A word heard a letter off is put right ("sampon" is Šampon), and the recognizers are told to expect your things by name.
- Settings, rethought after Safari, Chrome, Firefox, Arc, Brave, Edge and Orion: one screen of places, each with a mark and what it's set to (Search engine · Google, Tabs · After a day, Notify Me · 2 pages), opening a page of its own. Time saved stays on top, its breakdown a tap away. New: Clear browsing data (history, cookies and site data, cached files; the last hour, today or all time), Sites (every site treated differently, cookies accepted, blocking off, zoom, each reset with a swipe), Notifications (to-do alerts, nudges and Notify Me in one place, and a way to iOS Settings when they're off), and About (version, and what roko is built with). Shopping gets Forget What You Buy.
- A page that doesn't load says why, about the site by name, under roko's eclipse, whose inner disc slowly crosses while the page is down: "You're offline" (and it loads by itself when you're back online), "index.hr doesn't exist" (Search instead), "isn't answering", "is taking too long", "isn't safe to open" (no retry offered), "This page keeps stopping".
- A shopping list that sorts itself into aisles, in Croatian or English. Type "shopping" in the bar to open it, or "shopping milk, eggs" / "kupovina jabuke i deterdžent" to add straight from the bar. Things land in the order a shop is walked (fruit and vegetables, bakery, meat and fish, dairy and eggs, frozen, pantry, coffee and breakfast, sweets and snacks, drinks, household, personal care, baby, pets): "mlijeko" and "milk" are Dairy, "sok od naranče" and "orange juice" Drinks, "kokosovo mlijeko" Pantry, "hrana za pse" Pets. "mlijeko, jaja i kruh" is three things; "sok od naranče i jabuke" stays one. Words no rule knows go to Apple's on-device model, and an item you move to another aisle goes there from then on. It learns what you buy and how often: under the list, "You often buy" offers what's due again ("Usually every 4 days"), and typing offers what you've bought before, then everyday things (Croatian and English), then a shop's range ("jog" is Jogurt, then Danone Fantasia višnja jogurt): about 4,900 names, matched in under 3 ms a letter. In "mlijeko, jaja, kr", a suggestion finishes the last one. Tick things off as they go in the basket; they fold away under Bought. The list speaks English or Croatian (Settings → Today and to-dos → Shopping list language; Automatic follows the iPhone): "Voće i povrće", "Mliječni proizvodi i jaja", "Često kupuješ", "Obično svaka 3 dana". What you type is understood in both either way. It stays on this iPhone for now: sharing with family needs iCloud sharing, which comes with the paid Developer Program.
- To-dos sync with Apple Reminders, both ways (Settings → Today and to-dos → Sync with Reminders). They're kept the same as one Reminders list, a new "roko" list unless you choose another: added, renamed, moved to another day or time, ticked off or deleted on either side, it's so on the other within a moment. Reminders carries the list through iCloud to your Mac, iPad and Watch. When both sides changed the same to-do between syncs, Reminders wins; "this week" shows as the week's last day there. Turning it off unlinks without deleting anything.
- Home Screen widgets. **To-do** (small, medium, large): what's left for today, overdue first, each with its time, ticked off right on the widget (roko marks it done in its list the next time it's opened). **Weather and To-do** (medium: the weather beside the list; large: the weather across the top): the weather where you are and today's to-dos. **Answer** (small, medium, and on the Lock Screen): any answer you pinned in the bar, such as the weather in a town, a rate, days until, chosen in the widget's settings. The widgets read a small copy roko writes into an App Group whenever a to-do, the weather or a pinned answer changes, and only then; nothing runs in the background for them.
- Pay for street parking by text, in Osijek to start. Type "parking" (or "parkiranje") in the bar: roko finds the zone you're in from a precise location fix, shows its price and limit ("Zona 0 · 1,50 € an hour · 3 h a day"), and on a zone border offers both zones to choose from. Tapping it opens Messages with the zone's number and your registration written in; you send it. Once sent, the time left counts down on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, and at the top of the bar, with "Add an hour" for the next text, up to the zone's daily limit and never past the end of charging (21:00 weekdays, 15:00 Saturdays; Sundays and public holidays are free). A reminder comes 10 minutes before the end. Registrations go in Settings → Parking. Outside the cities roko knows, nothing appears. Zone data: OpenStreetMap, built by Scripts/build_parking.py.
- Private browsing says so where you look: on a private tab the bar's capsule reads "Private" beside roko's closed eye instead of +, since tapping it opens a private search. The rest of the bar stays as it is.
- Settings opens on what roko has saved you: the time in large type, then where it came from, each with its count. Cookie pop-ups answered (with the clicks they took), pages read as summaries, Browse for Me answers, and answers in the bar (weather, sums, rates, time zones, the next event). The times are estimates, said plainly under them: 3 seconds a click, reading at 230 words a minute, a search plus a minute for each page Browse for Me read, 10 to 20 seconds for an answer in the bar. Only counts are kept, on this iPhone; long-press the total to start counting again. It costs nothing while browsing: a few counters written when something is saved, and a word count when a summary finishes (under a millisecond for a long article).
- Six more app icons in Settings → App icon: the "ro" monogram, the octagonal O and the "roko" wordmark, each in orange on near-black and near-black on orange.
- Answer language now has a default plus languages kept as they are, on one page (Settings → Answer language): Write in picks the language summaries, Browse for Me and Notify Me write in, and under it, Keep as they are lists every language to tick the others you read (shown even while Write in is Automatic, so it's clear more than one can be chosen): a page in your language or a kept one is summarized in its own language, anything else in yours. Croatian with English kept: a Croatian or English article stays as it is, a German one comes in Croatian.
- Settings, rethought: grouped by what you do (Address bar, Voice, Pages, Today and to-dos, Summaries and answers, Privacy and sync), with Block, Notify Me, Shared pages and the app icon on pages of their own so the main screen stays short. New switches for features added lately: Hold + to speak, which languages dictation listens for (Croatian and English, or one), ticks as the bar's rows go by, pull further to summarize, and alerts before a to-do's time.
- A question said while holding + ("koji su rezultati izbora u srbiji", "what's the best time to visit Rovinj") goes straight to Browse for Me; a site's name opens it; anything else lands in the bar.
- Remove pages from history right in the address bar: swipe a page from your history to the left (a trash mark comes in behind it; past a third of the way, let go), or long-press it for Remove from History. On a site's row (index.hr itself) it's Remove Site from History, every page of it.
- Hold + to speak. The address bar opens out of it, listening, with the words coming into the field as they're heard and the rows following; let go and it's done. English and Croatian are heard at once, each by its own recognizer on the same sound, and the clearer hearing wins, so there's no language to pick. Said as an address ("index točka hr", "theverge dot com slash tech", "w w w dot bbc dot com") or as "open"/"otvori" and a site ("otvori index", "open youtube"), a site roko knows opens at once, matched to your history and well-known sites ("glas slavonije točka hr" is glas-slavonije.hr). Anything else goes into the bar as typed, its best row ready: a to-do ("sutra nazvati mamu u 5", "remind me to pay rent on friday"), a question for Browse for Me, a search. A day said first is read too ("sutra nazvati mamu"). The microphone is on only while + is held.
- The address bar knows a to-do when it sees one: "call Petra tomorrow", "kupiti mlijeko", "remind me to pay rent friday", "dentist at 15" put the to-do first (titled as it will be added, "Pay rent, for Friday"), so Return adds it, as Browse for Me goes first for a question. It reads doing words in English and Croatian, "remind me to" and "ne zaboravi", and words followed by a day or time; questions and look-ups ("how to pay rent", "weather tomorrow") stay searches.
- Scrolling the address bar's list ticks for each row that passes under the field, like a picker wheel, while it's dragged or still gliding.
- Typing an address, the site comes first: "index" offers index.hr itself, its front page, ahead of the articles you read on it, whether in history or open in tabs; the articles follow below. A page open in several tabs is one row.
- The launcher's field goes to the top of the screen, as in Arc Search: it grows out of the bar's capsule and rises there in a fifth of a second, then the rows come in under it, down to the keyboard. Its place no longer depends on the keyboard, so a taller one (SwiftKey) or one that reports its size late can't make it jump.
- The launcher grows out of the bar for real: its field starts exactly on the capsule you tapped and widens and rises into place, with the keyboard coming up under it, then sinks back into the capsule on close. (The capsule used to fade out while a field appeared at the top: the frames it measured included its own movement, so it never moved.)
- To-do's field: the chip says when in short ("Wed 15:00") and is a menu (Today, Tomorrow, This Week, Someday, Date and Time…) for when the words don't say. "at 3" means 15:00 (1 to 6 are the afternoon), and a day typed first ("Wednesday at 3") is read too.

### Removed
- Five app icons: the blue, green, yellow and grey stars, and the orange chevrons.

### Fixed
- Big numbers lead only where someone came for them: a recipe's time, servings and difficulty, a price, a distance. News and essays open on their gist ("2028" over a story about a football club's US partnership is gone), an explanation gets one only when a number is the answer, and a year or a date never shows as one.
- A recipe summary no longer leads with an ingredient ("350g pasta", "3 eggs"): a big number that repeats a line of the ingredient list, by name or by amount, is left to the list.
- A short question is answered in the phone's language unless it's clearly another: "cast iron vs nonstick pan" came back in Vietnamese, since a few words are too little to tell a language by. Under 120 characters, the guess leans on the phone's languages and needs 90% confidence to leave them.
- Answers read more cleanly. A sentence that repeats the one before it (60% of its words the same) is dropped. A checklist's note ("for frying", "optional") sits under the item instead of out on the right, and an amount the item already says isn't shown twice. Side-by-side tables keep their value columns the same width, left-aligned, with headings level at the bottom. Steps and checklists have no icon by their heading, so it doesn't compete with step 1. Source numbers take the page type's colour (grey on a general answer) instead of orange on every band.
- Browse for Me shows pictures only when they're photos that loaded: logos, placeholders, wordmarks and text banners (fewer than 60 colours on a small thumbnail) are left out, and with none left the row isn't shown.
- A page type the model writes on a line of its own, or runs into the next line, is read as the type instead of showing as text.
- Hiding rules now win over a site's own styles: each is scoped one step tighter, so a site that shows its pop-up with !important (The Independent's membership offer) can't undo it. The Independent's membership pop-up and newsletter banner are hidden.
- A summary that one engine starts and another finishes keeps its words on screen until the new ones arrive, instead of going blank. The summary's roko signature now ends the summary, as on Browse for Me's answers.
- Searches open in the phone's language and region: Google got `hl` and `gl`, so an English phone on a Croatian network no longer sees Google in Croatian.
- Browse for Me no longer cites affiliate content farms: expired domains reused for "15 best headphones" pages (requiemforadream.com and the like). Claude's search is told never to return them (`blocked_domains`), both engines drop them from the sources if one slips through, and Claude is asked to prefer makers, established reviewers and major publications and skip pages that only exist to earn commissions. The same question now answers from Android Authority, RTINGS and recordingnow.
- Browse for Me no longer waits for ever. Apple's on-device model sometimes went quiet without failing (seen in the simulator after other requests): no words, no error, "Writing your answer" for minutes. Now 30 seconds without a word ends it, and it says so, with Try again under it. Summaries get the same limit.
- When Apple's model fails, Browse for Me and summaries say how ("declined to summarize this page", "too much to read at once", "busy") instead of "needs Apple Intelligence" or "The model refused to answer."; without a Claude key, they say a key would take over.
- Browse for Me's own search asks DuckDuckGo for the phone's region, and keeps one page per site, so one site no longer fills most of the sources.
- Search suggestions are asked for in the phone's language and region (Google's hl and gl, DuckDuckGo's kl), not wherever the network is: "new york" no longer suggested "new yorker osijek" on an American phone.
- A site open in a tab is one row in the bar, not two ("Apple, Already open" and "Apple, Open"), and the same page under two addresses is one row in the recent list; removing that row from history removes both.
- Opening the address bar no longer flashes orange at the + capsule (the hold-to-speak fill started at touch-down, so every tap showed a sliver of it; it now starts after 120 ms), and its rows come in just behind the dimming instead of ahead of it, so text never sits on the bare page. The Shopping row is gone from the address bar's list, like its pin.
- The address bar opens in place: the field fades in at the top and settles a few points, the keyboard rising with it, instead of flying up from the bottom bar. On a warm open it starts appearing 38 ms after the tap (was 70) and has the keyboard at 39 ms (was 125); its rows are built once it's showing.
- Opening the address bar with a copied link offered above the bar threw the link away instead of listing it in the bar.
- A tab whose page had failed to load came back blank after a relaunch, loading nothing and showing no error: it's loaded again.
- Shopping: picking a suggestion with "i" in its name ("Ahmad Čaj menta i limun") no longer adds two things; a decimal comma ("Dukat mlijeko 2,8%") no longer splits one; Done tapped as a spoken list finishes can't add it twice; listening stops on its own after the words stop changing, by 45 seconds at most, and when Shopping closes; ticking, deleting and moving can't touch an item already deleted; a thing already on another shop's list says which; tapping another shop's name clears the move offer; aisles are remembered rather than worked out on every redraw, and the catalogue is matched without allocating on every letter.
- Notify Me: a watch that becomes readable without scripts, or was read by an older version, is read afresh instead of compared, which could stop it for good or send a false alert.
- The offline error screen no longer says "Back online" before the connection has come back, and loads at once after a blip; one connection monitor for the app instead of one per redraw; its eclipse draws at 12 frames a second and stops while covered.
- A secure-connection failure (often a captive portal) offers Try again instead of calling the site unsafe. Resetting a site in Settings → Sites applies to open tabs, and the Sites count is read when Settings opens, not on every redraw.
- The launcher opens and closes about twice as fast (0.11 s instead of 0.2), so it reads as the bar becoming the launcher rather than a flight.
- The launcher's field flew up from the bar as an empty dark pill, its contents shown only once it landed: they now ride along at their own size, uncovered as the field widens. And an offer above the bar (cookies, a copied link) no longer lingers as a blank box when the bar opens; it's put away first.
- The link you copied is offered above the bar only when roko can show which link it is; the bare Paste button stays in the address bar's list. "Page not working? Allow cookies" now takes three reloads within 45 seconds, not two in two minutes (following a live blog set it off).
- Shopping's field no longer autocorrects: "šampon, brufen" became "Sampson, burden".
- index.hr's cookie pop-up stayed up in private tabs. ### Performance
- Blocking lists half the size and quicker to load, blocking the same: ad and tracker servers that no longer exist are dropped (17,617, checked against two public resolvers), plain host rules ship as a list of hosts made into rules only when compiling, and hiding rules ship as lines. In the app: 16 MB of lists is now 7.4 MB, the compiled lists 49 MB are 38.6 MB, compiling after an update is about 15% quicker, and the hiding indexes load in a third of the time.
- The light's grain is made from random bytes in well under a millisecond, instead of 90 ms through Core Image: a hitch the first time a light showed. The pull's light is drawn off the main thread after the first page; the first pull took 92 ms to draw it, now 0.2 ms.
- The first page after launch starts about 0.16 s sooner: it waits only for the request rules; the hiding indexes now decode in parallel.
- Tab saves are batched and no longer run on every page load; tab pictures compress off the main thread.
- Page loads skip resending unchanged blocker settings to WebKit.
- Site icons are no longer re-read from disk on every redraw.
- Scrolling no longer creates a timer per frame; the empty-ad-box check waits for scrolling to pause.
- Browse for Me is two to three times faster with Claude (measured on an iPhone): a deep question 48 s to 20 s, at about half the tokens; an ordinary one 29 s to 8 to 13 s, on Haiku, with the answer streaming in from about 6 s. Sonnet uses the basic search tool instead of the one that filters results by running code, searches less, and thinks at medium effort.
