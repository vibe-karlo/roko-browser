Questions.

Is it free?

The beta on TestFlight is free.

How do I install it?

TestFlight is Apple's app for betas. Open the beta link on your iPhone, install TestFlight if you don't have it, then install roko from there.

What does roko know about me?

There's no account, no ads and no tracking of its own. The privacy policy lists everything that leaves your iPhone.

Which iPhones does it run on?

Any iPhone on iOS 27 or later. Without Apple Intelligence, summaries and answers need your own Claude API key.

Does it work on iPad?

Not yet. roko is iPhone only for now.

Does it work on iPhone Duo?

Yes. Split View parts at the fold, and the controls stay off it. Prop it open like a small laptop and the page stands above, with a trackpad for it below.

Which AI does it use?

Apple's, with no account. Summaries are written on your iPhone. Browse for Me searches the web through DuckDuckGo, reads the pages, then writes its answer on your iPhone too.

You can add your own Claude API key. Browse for Me then uses Claude, and so do summaries Apple's model can't write well. That text goes to Anthropic under your key, and Anthropic bills you for it.

Can it be my default browser?

Not yet. That needs Apple's approval, and so do passkeys and passwords matched to the site you're on. We're asking.

Is it open source?

Yes. The app, its server and this site are on GitHub, under the GPL-3.0 licence.

A site isn't working right.

Open the page menu and tap "Ads, trackers and pop-ups blocked". That site goes back to the original, and every other site stays clean.
