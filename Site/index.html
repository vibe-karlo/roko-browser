Questions.

Is it free?

The beta on TestFlight is free.

What does it know about me?

roko has no account, no ads and no tracking of its own. The privacy policy lists everything that leaves your iPhone.

Which iPhones does it work on?

iPhones running iOS 27 or later. Summaries and answers use Apple Intelligence where your iPhone has it. You can also add your own Claude API key.

Can it be my default browser?

Not yet. Default browsers need Apple's permission, and so do passkeys and site-matched passwords. Those requests come next.

A site isn't working right.

Open the page menu and tap the blocking card. That site goes back to the original, and every other site stays clean.
